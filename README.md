# Emerald Strategy

Role & Aesthetic

You are a world-class UI/UX designer and frontend engineer. Build a premium, minimalist, and highly polished personal portfolio website. The aesthetic must mirror the high-end, intentional design of Matthew Encina and Mod Musings. Use the exact typography style of Mod Musings—a pristine, clean sans-serif (like Inter or Neue Montreal) for the body text, paired with a highly refined serif (like PP Editorial New or Playfair Display) for impactful headings. Keep the layout airy, with generous whitespace, and absolutely no clutter.




Colors & Styling




Base: Pristine white and ultra-light off-white for main content areas.

Accent Color: Vibrant Orange (#DB5D11) for highlights, specific heading words, and primary buttons.

Gradients:




Call-to-Action blocks: A premium Emerald Green to Black gradient.

Footer: A smooth, cinematic gradient transitioning from solid Black to #DB5D11.

Components: Buttons must be pill-shaped. Information cards must feature very subtle, delicate borders with soft rounded corners.

Architecture & Content Strategy

Create a seamless, four-section architecture (Home, About, Work, Resources) incorporating the following details:




Home: A bold, minimalist hero section introducing an ecosystem builder and strategy consultant who engineers scalable operational systems. Below this, include a "Mission & Vision" section using side-by-side cards on an off-white background. Highlight key words in the serif headings using #DB5D11.

About: Keep the copy sharp. Focus on a proven track record of scaling complex initiatives from zero to one in Nairobi, managing strategic brand architecture, and driving high-value institutional partnerships.

Work: Showcase premium physical and digital infrastructure projects. Include a dedicated, dark-themed banner utilizing the Emerald Green to Black gradient. The banner should feature white serif text reading "Shape the room. Become a partner." alongside a #DB5D11 pill-shaped button.

Resources: Design a sleek, stark black section offering operational templates. Use white serif typography for the description and a solid white pill button for the download trigger.

Footer: Implement the Black to #DB5D11 gradient background, featuring clean navigation links on the left and a Nairobi base location tag on the right.
